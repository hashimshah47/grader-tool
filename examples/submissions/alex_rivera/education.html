Education

I am currently enrolled in the Bachelor of Computer Science program at the University of Windsor. My favourite courses so far have been Introduction to the Internet, where I learned how browsers, servers and markup work together, and Data Structures, which changed the way I think about organising information. Before university I completed my high school diploma with honours, and I spent two summers volunteering as a math tutor at the public library. Teaching younger students showed me how important patience and clear examples are when you are learning something new for the first time.
